Print Care · Application information

PrintCare ERP

A printing-workflow application with a read-only Google Drive connection for finding approved documents, previewing PDFs and downloading files needed for work.

Find approved files

Read file lists and document metadata from approved folders in the connected Google Drive account.

Preview PDFs

View existing PDF pages in the ERP before choosing a file for your work.

Download a copy

Download an existing file to your device. The read-only connection does not edit, upload, move or delete the original Drive file.

Why Google access is requested

Google sign-in identifies the authorized account. Read-only Drive access lets the ERP retrieve existing documents for its file-list, preview and download features. Access is granted through Google's consent screen.

The Google authorization scopes are openid, email and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly. Google describes the Drive permission as the ability to see and download Drive files; the ERP workflow presents its approved file list.

For authorized use

PrintCare ERP is used in the operator's controlled environment. This public website explains the application; it does not provide access to ERP records or Google Drive files. Contact the administrator for application access.
